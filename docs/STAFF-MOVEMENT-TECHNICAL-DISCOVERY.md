# Staff Movement — technical discovery

Historical discovery completed 26 November 2026. The [current specification and migration record](STAFF-MOVEMENT-SPEC.md) documents the implemented foundation, generated active-request index and local verification. The approved [full product requirements](STAFF-MOVEMENT-REQUIREMENTS.md) are authoritative. This document distinguishes repository observations from recommendations and decisions; the observations below describe the pre-implementation repository. Discovery is based on source and documentation inspection, not a database inventory or end-to-end execution. Existing paused regression and release/security blockers remain in [PROJECT-TODO.md](PROJECT-TODO.md).

## Existing implementation and reusable infrastructure

- No staff movement, deployment, redeployment, new-staff request or movement-history workflow, API, or page was found in the inspected source. `orbit360-FE/src/components/EmployeeGate.jsx` contains `request_staff_movement` and `approve_staff_movement` in an old client-side list; its gate returns children and the backend `src/features/permissions/index.ts` contains neither permission. These strings do not establish an implemented permission or workflow. Recruitment has job postings and applications (`src/features/recruitment/` and `orbit360-FE/src/components/recruitment/`), not an identified new-staff requisition workflow.
- `src/features/employee/employee.model.ts`, `employee-schema.ts`, `employee.service.ts`, `employee-draft.model.ts`, and `employee-change-request.model.ts` supply employee records and an existing maker/checker maintenance pattern. `approveMaintenance` rejects the same requester/checker and conditionally updates the pending request within a Sequelize transaction; it is a pattern, not a configurable multi-stage movement engine.
- `src/pending-authorization/pending-authorization.repository.ts` aggregates explicitly coded, workflow-specific queues and filters; `orbit360-FE/src/pages/authorization-center/` displays them. Extend these entry points for eligible movement actions; keep the movement service as the source of truth and enforce the same access in its APIs. Do not duplicate approval actions in a second approval engine or assume this aggregate already supports arbitrary stages.
- Authentication and `JobRolePermissions` provide permission checks (`src/features/authentication/`, `src/features/permissions/`, `src/utils/check-permission.ts`). `Employee.supervisorId` links a reporting manager. A supervisor is identified by reporting relationships in `auth.controller.ts`; no department-head assignment or delegation mechanism was found in the inspected models. A supervisor and HOD must not be assumed equivalent. Frontend `src/pages/Layout.jsx` recognizes HR Manager by a job-role title regex for navigation; this does not establish Head of HR authority. Executive, IT and Administration approver identities or delegation are not established by the inspected permissions. Payroll permissions exist, but they do not identify a specific officer or prove an assignment policy.
- Existing role notification infrastructure is in `src/features/notifications/`, with strict event definitions, recipient resolution, preference channels, polling, dedupe and after-commit dispatch documented in [NOTIFICATIONS.md](NOTIFICATIONS.md). The workflow recipient resolver supports persisted assignment, supervisor and permission references; employee-to-user linking uses email. It skips unresolved or ineligible recipients; do not infer an HOD from a supervisor or broadcast on uncertainty. Delivery is best effort and is not a durable outbox.
- Document endpoints and model in `src/features/hr-document/` support uploads and public/private access, but the observed model does not bind documents to an employee. Uploads are saved under `public/documents` and `src/app.ts` serves `/documents` with `express.static`; controller-level access filtering alone is therefore insufficient evidence of protected file downloads. Do not store confidential movement letters there as presently exposed. An owner/participant-scoped authenticated download and protected storage are required and must be verified. No movement template, authorized signatory, document versioning or PDF generator was identified in the inspected implementation. Nodemailer/Handlebars mail utilities exist (`src/utils/mail.util.ts`, `src/utils/email.service.ts`); approved templates, signatory and delivery policy are still required.
- No job scheduler, background queue or durable scheduled-execution mechanism was found in inspected source and package dependencies. Interview scheduling records a date, not an execution worker. Sequelize transaction and conditional-update examples exist, but reliable execution at a future date, recovery and monitoring are new work.

## Proposed lifecycle and authorization

Use a movement-owned state machine and audit trail integrated with the existing authentication, permission checks, Authorization Center and notification service, not a replacement for the application's other approval flows. Final technical status names and transition guards belong in the specification. Each transition verifies the persisted current state/version, actor assignment and organizational scope, and writes actor, UTC time, decision, reason and safe change history transactionally.

1. Employee, assigned current/receiving HOD, or authorized HR initiates. Snapshot current and proposed assignment and prevent conflicting active requests for the employee. When a current/receiving HOD initiates, capture that person's corresponding endorsement at submission; do not require that same endorsement again. HR preliminary review remains required. Whether and when a changed assignment invalidates that endorsement must be specified.
2. Assign HR preliminary validation; forward, return or decline with reasons. Explicitly record unavailable position/headcount/competency evidence as a blocker, not an implicit pass.
3. Request current HOD endorsement, conditional release or objection; record conditions and route objection to authorized HR escalation rather than automatically closing it. Obtain receiving HOD acceptance, modification request or reasoned decline. Resolve missing, conflicting and acting assignments through approved policy.
4. HR issues final recommendation, then assigned Head of HR decides; configurable executive approvers participate when the approved matrix requires them. Return, clarification, rejection and cancellation are explicit, audited transitions. Makers cannot independently final-approve their own request, including through delegation; check subject/approver conflicts as policy requires.
5. Following all approvals, generate a versioned letter with approved content/signatory and deliver via access-checked employee-specific storage. Capture acknowledgement separately from legally relevant consent; do not infer consent from a read or acknowledgement. Model mandatory clearances as independently assigned tasks and an authorized exception process.
6. Schedule implementation for the effective date only after all mandatory gates. A transaction atomically updates supported assignment fields, closes/opens assignment history and marks the movement completed. For future or independently authorized modules, record pending separately authorized actions rather than claiming a cross-system atomic update. Failures become visible, auditable implementation failures; never report completion for partial work. Temporary expiry prompts an approved review/return workflow; formal reversal is a new authorized action, not history deletion.
7. Material amendment invalidates affected approvals and letter versions and requires renewed authorization before execution. Returned and re-reviewed events need distinct occurrence identifiers; conditional writes prevent competing decisions from producing misleading notifications.

Recommended data concepts for a later specification: movement request/reference and version, immutable current/proposed/approved snapshots, decision/audit history, assignment/delegation and versioned approval matrix, clearance tasks/exceptions, employee acknowledgement and separately recorded consent, protected letter versions, execution attempts and effective-dated employee assignment history. Schema design and migrations require normal review and approval; none are created in discovery. Reference generation must be unique under concurrency and preserve the PRD format where configured.

## Scheduling and integrity

The future specification should define an Africa/Lagos business-date boundary, UTC audit timestamps and an explicit handling rule for past dates. A durable database-backed schedule and recoverable execution process can use the existing database/transaction stack, but the deployment mechanism, operational ownership and any dependency must be approved before implementation. On execution, claim work with a transactional lock/conditional version guard, re-read current employee state and approved snapshot, clearance, consent and authorization, and reject/review stale or conflicting changes. One active conflicting movement per employee needs race-safe database enforcement or equivalent locking, not only a UI check. Idempotent execution keys, bounded retries, monitoring and a manual recovery path prevent duplicate updates; record failures and alert the accountable operator. A process-local timer alone cannot guarantee execution after restart or across multiple instances.

## Notifications and frontend

Propose a dedicated Staff Movement category, subject to category/preferences and migration approval. Define strict registry payloads and role-scoped destinations; no comments, compensation, reasons, confidential attachments or sensitive employment details in messages. Event examples, subject to policy approval: submission to the assigned HR officer; validation to current HOD; endorsement to receiving HOD; reviews complete to HR; final recommendation to assigned Head of HR; approval to employee and both eligible HODs; letter available to employee; clearance task to each assignee; approaching effective date, overdue action and temporary expiry to explicitly assigned stakeholders; completion to authorized participants. Requester outcome and cancellation notices require a separately approved recipient matrix. A non-applicable prior endorsement must advance to the next real assignee, without duplicate notifications. Do not invent recipients or leak employee identity to unauthorized queues.

Integrate each event with its corresponding phase using `NotificationService.notifyAfterCommit` where transactional, existing recipient resolution, preferences, polling/acknowledgement, unread count and self-toast suppression. Use occurrence/version-aware recipient dedupe keys; check access at destination and handle revoked or missing assignments explicitly. Reminder scheduling requires the durable execution mechanism above; after-commit notification is best effort and cannot by itself guarantee a reminder. No new notification service or tables per module.

For the frontend, recommend one role-aware Staff Movement landing page with contextual tabs for requests, assigned actions, clearance, history and reports/configuration only for authorized roles; employee self-service shows own requests, incoming movement, letters and assigned clearance. Executive/HOD/HR actions should also appear in the existing Authorization Center where appropriate. Avoid a long sidebar and preserve HR Resources expanded/Employee Portal collapsed for HR and direct navigation for employees/supervisors. Existing navigation and design tokens live in `orbit360-FE/src/pages/Layout.jsx` and `DESIGN.md`; use existing `src/components/system/` patterns. Visibility is not backend authorization.

## Earlier scope reconciliation

Request Deployment and Redeployment relate to movements of existing employees and can be represented as configured categories/initiator flows, subject to terminology confirmation. Employee concurrence must distinguish acknowledgement of receipt from separately required contractual consent. Request New Staff is not expressly a request to move an existing employee: recommend a separate workforce requisition feeding Recruitment, with shared approved-position/headcount data when available. Retain it in the backlog until the product owner decides its ownership, approval flow and relationship to Staff Movement; do not silently drop or implement it.

## Decisions requiring product confirmation

The approved PRD already establishes initiation channels, HR coordination, routine Head of HR approval as a configurable default, HOD submission endorsements, acknowledgement/consent separation, clearance and effective-date controls. The following are unresolved implementation policies, not a request to reapprove those decisions:

1. **Identity and delegation:** recommend explicit, effective-dated current/receiving HOD, HR and executive assignments with authorized delegates and fail-closed missing assignments; supervisor and job-title text are insufficient. Confirm assignment ownership, scope and maker/checker conflicts.
2. **Approval matrix and exceptions:** recommend versioned, administratively configured rules with controlled changes, explicit executive triggers, SLA/escalation, clearance exception and amendment thresholds. Confirm authorities, defaults, cancellation and appeal/objection resolution.
3. **Data and headcount:** recommend define legal employer versus Company, branch/location/grade/cost centre, approved positions, headcount and competencies before mandating automated validation. Confirm sources and whether missing data blocks a request or is documented for authorized exception.
4. **Timing, history and temporary return:** recommend Lagos calendar date, execution only after mandatory readiness, stale-record review rather than overwrite, approval for reversal/return, and explicit handling of overdue/past dates and active requests. Confirm policies, expiry, existing pending approvals and initial-history cutoff.
5. **Confidential letters, consent and delivery:** recommend protected owner-specific document access, approved template/signatory, separate explicit consent where legally required and minimal notification content. Confirm consent triggers, retention, email configuration and permissible recipients.
6. **Access and compensation:** recommend separately authorized access and payroll changes with no automatic privilege grants, as the PRD requires. Confirm approval boundaries, effective period, cross-entity/statutory checks and failure handling.
7. **Request New Staff:** recommend workforce requisition connected to Recruitment rather than an employee transfer; confirm owner, scope and sequencing.
8. **Notifications and operations:** recommend a dedicated category and assigned recipients, no broadcast, durable execution with operational ownership and explicit retries; confirm category, recipient matrix, reminder thresholds and delivery reliability requirements.

## Recommended implementation phases and acceptance

1. Produce a single technical specification and approved decision matrix; resolve data sources, authorizers, secure documents, operations and dependent permissions. Preserve existing release blockers.
2. Add approved organizational assignment/configuration, core request lifecycle, scope checks, concurrency-safe audit and Authorization Center integration. Test all initiating roles, maker/checker and HOD-at-submission cases, returns, competing approvals and missing assignments.
3. Add protected approved letters, separate acknowledgement/consent, independently assigned clearance and transactional phase notifications. Test unauthorized access, privacy, preferences, duplicate/retry, rollback and revoked access.
4. Add reliable scheduled implementation, immutable history, monitoring and recovery; test future dates, missing clearance, record changes, concurrent workers, rollback, idempotent retries, amendments, temporary expiry and formal reversal.
5. Add role-scoped dashboard, reports and exports, and only approved integrations with payroll, access, leave and training. Defer KPI/performance/appraisal as above. Run automated and role-based browser acceptance, responsive and accessibility checks before claiming completion.
